Thank you for reviewing Spoonerism. We retested this same build on a clean device end to end. Every screen and control behaved as designed; we could not reproduce a crash, a dead end, an empty screen, or a non-functioning feature.

Three behaviours are deliberate. A quick pass can mistake them for defects.

1. After a search that finds nothing, Browse may show "Prado had no match. The bundled shelf is here." while the list still shows bundled works. That is not a blank failure — pick a row from the shelf to save, or try "Search a work" again.
2. Undo (accessibility "Undo") stays disabled, and the app may say "Nothing to undo.", until a hit or miss exists. Record a live tap first, then undo from Quiz or from Settings under "This device".
3. "Reset all data" clears works, hits, and misses on this device and returns to the three-page cover on purpose. Confirm "Reset all data?" with the destructive "Reset all data", or choose "Cancel".

Full review path. No account, no login:
- Cold launch shows a full-screen splash with no text. The system may ask for notification permission. If onboarding is unfinished, the three-page cover appears (light interface): top-right "Skip" on pages 1–2, bottom "Continue". Page 1: "Save a painting." Page 2: "Tap a swapped word." Page 3: "File the painting." "Skip" or finishing page 3 opens Quiz.
- On Quiz, if empty, tap "Browse". In the "Browse" sheet, use "Search a work" or the bundled shelf; tap a row to keep a work on this device. Close back to Quiz.
- When Ready ("Pick a painting" / "Maker or title will paint as words."), tap "Spoon". When Live ("Tap the swapped word"), tap the word that swapped its first letter. A true tap files the painting; a miss greys that word.
- Open "Kept" for "Filed" and "Hits and misses". Open "How it works" for the rules, then "Try a word" to return. Open "Settings" for "This device" counts, "Re-run onboarding", "Reset all data", and "Contact".

The bundled Browse shelf includes Prado works such as Las Meninas, The Third of May 1808, and The Garden of Earthly Delights when search is empty or fails. On a physical device there is no planted play state — start from onboarding and Browse. Kept works, live or filed lines, hits, misses, and onboarding completion resume after relaunch; backgrounding saves progress.

Technical facts: no accounts, no in-app purchases, no ads, no analytics, no user-generated content, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Notifications may be requested at cold launch via the system dialog; there is no custom usage-description string in the app. Camera is not requested (a usage string "This app does not use the camera." is present in build settings, but no camera prompt appears). Works, hits, and misses stay on this device. English UI only; counts and day stamps use the device locale’s number formatting. Portrait only on iPhone and iPad; minimum iOS 17.0.
